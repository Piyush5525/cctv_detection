# Demo clips check: what the real detectors do on the provided clips

**Tiny sample, one clip per class. Not accuracy.** Read-only: no tuning, no incidents created, nothing sent. Each clip was replayed through the real detectors and the same `EventCapturePipeline` as the live cameras (`api/services/clip_replay.py`, video time, sampling as live), with all five detectors enabled on every clip so cross-triggers are visible. Generated by `scripts/demo_clips_check.py`; the clips themselves are not part of the repository.

Thresholds in force: fall score 0.6, violence CLIP 0.22, snatch score 0.7, fire/crash floor 0.5. 'Seconds to detect' is the video time at which the event was confirmed (N of M frames), from clip start.
